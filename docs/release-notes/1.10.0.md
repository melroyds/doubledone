# 1.10.0 release notes (iOS and Android)

*One file per version. Every App Store localisation has its own What's New and nothing inherits it; Play takes
ONE paste with six locale tags, each block at most 500 characters. Spanish is one neutral text under both `es-419`
and `es-ES`. Every block was checked by script (counts, no dash, digit, exclamation, currency or percentage, no
Premium, price, free, review, rating or website, French no-break spaces, real diacritics) and natively read.*

**Native read: done (2026-10-25).** A translator and an independent native reader per language, each checking the UI
names against that language's catalogue, plus an English truth check against the code. German and Italian were
approved as written. French: "Pose le doigt", not "Laisse un doigt posé" (the first step of an instruction), and
"si tu t'absentes", not the calque "t'éloignes". Spanish: "que tiene su propia pantalla", not "en una pantalla
propia", which could read as "a screen of your own". The App Review paragraph was tightened by the truth check:
who can get a month (no Premium of any kind), the real floor (two words or one long one), 30 days, "the same way
as" the card-free month, and where the offer shows.

What 1.10.0 carries to the phones over 1.9.1: drag a task by its grip (`865d1f6`) and "A month on us" phase 1
(`be22a5c`), both live on the web since 2026-10-25 (decision log). The month is deliberately absent from the
store notes (they never name Premium); App Review hears about it below. A new version string because 1.9.1 is in
App Review and its train closes to new builds once it is approved.

### English (333 characters)

```
Move a task by its grip. Rest a finger on the six dots at a task's right edge, then drag it where you want it. Holding anywhere else on the task still opens its card, and Move up and Move down are still there. You can also write to us from Settings, under Send feedback, on a screen of its own that keeps your words if you step away.
```

### German (463 characters)

```
Verschieb eine Aufgabe jetzt an ihrem Griff. Leg einen Finger auf die sechs Punkte am rechten Rand einer Aufgabe und zieh sie dorthin, wo du sie haben möchtest. Hältst du die Aufgabe an einer anderen Stelle gedrückt, öffnet sich wie bisher ihre Karte, und „Nach oben“ und „Nach unten“ sind weiterhin da. Außerdem kannst du uns in den Einstellungen unter „Feedback senden“ schreiben, auf einer eigenen Seite, die deine Nachricht behält, auch wenn du kurz weggehst.
```

### Spanish, neutral, for Spanish (Mexico) and Spanish (Spain) (381 characters)

```
Ahora puedes mover una tarea arrastrándola. Apoya un dedo en los seis puntos de su borde derecho y llévala adonde quieras. Si la mantienes presionada en cualquier otro lugar, se abre su tarjeta como siempre, y «Subir» y «Bajar» siguen ahí. También puedes escribirnos desde Ajustes, en «Enviar comentarios», que tiene su propia pantalla y guarda lo que escribes si sales un momento.
```

## Apple, App Review Information and notes

Sign-in required: **Yes**. User name `appreview@doubledone.app`. Password: `No password: one-time code, see notes`.
1.9.1's notes with the version paragraph replaced. 3894 characters, under App Store Connect's 4,000.

```
DoubleDone is a calm daily to-do app for people who find a full task list overwhelming. The daily loop is free and needs no account. Premium adds optional extras.

Version 1.10.0 adds two things. A task on Today can now be moved by dragging the grip at its right edge; Move up and Move down stay on the task's card. And a note to us has its own screen (Settings > Send feedback). As a thank-you, a signed-in user with no Premium of any kind (no subscription and no free month running) may be given one month (30 days) of Premium for a note of two or more words (or one long word), at most once every 180 days. The offer shows as a line under Send feedback, and once as a card on Today a week after this version is first opened, only when our server says the signed-in account can get one, or to someone not signed in, where it says the month follows sign-in. The month is granted on our server to the signed-in account, the same way as the existing card-free month: there is no code and nothing to redeem, nothing renews, nothing is ever charged, and it ends by itself. It is never tied to a rating or a review: nothing in the app asks for one in return, and the rating card stays off while such a month runs. With AI on, and only while a month is on offer, Claude checks the note once for spam before a month is given (only a clear spam verdict stops it), and the screen says so above Send. After a note is sent, an optional link opens a public Google Calendar booking page for a 20-minute call with the developer. The review account already has Premium, so its note screen says "Notes are always welcome" and offers no month; signed out, the screen says a month follows sign-in.

RATING CARD
The app asks for the App Store's rating card only through StoreKit's own API (requestReview), with no prompt, button or words of our own before or after it. It asks at most once per app version, at least 180 days apart, after a user closes a good day. It needs five closed days spread over at least two weeks, so it will not appear during review.

REVIEW ACCOUNT (sign-in is passwordless)
1. On Today, tap "Sync and sharing".
2. Enter appreview@doubledone.app and tap "Email me a code".
3. In any browser, open https://api.doubledone.app/review-code. Use the 6-digit code on the line that starts "appreview@doubledone.app:".
4. Type it into the app. Codes last an hour. If none shows, tap send again in the app and refresh the page.
This account already has Premium, so every Premium feature is open.

TO TEST THE IN-APP PURCHASE, PLEASE DO IT SIGNED OUT
The review account already has Premium, so it never sees the purchase screen. Buying needs no account: before signing in (or after Settings > Sign out), open Menu at the top right of Today, scroll to the end of the page, tap Premium, choose Monthly or Annual, and tap Go Premium. "Restore a purchase" is on the same screen.

AI AND THIRD-PARTY DISCLOSURE
Sort for me, Break it down and Scan send the text or photo the user chooses to Anthropic's Claude, only when the user taps one of them. A line naming Anthropic's Claude sits beside those buttons, the welcome offers an AI-free choice before anything runs, and Settings turns AI off entirely.

SHARED LIST (OURS)
A user can share one list with one other person by reading them a six-character code. There is no feed, discovery, profile or public content. "Report this list" and "Leave this list" are on the Ours screen, and we can disable any list server-side.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Settings > "Delete account and data". An Apple subscription is managed in the Apple ID settings, and the app says so before deleting.

DoubleDone is designed with ADHD, autistic and OCD users in mind, and its name now says who it is for. It is a to-do list, not medical care: it makes no medical, diagnostic or treatment claim, collects no health data, and its Terms of Use say so in a "Not medical care" section.
```
