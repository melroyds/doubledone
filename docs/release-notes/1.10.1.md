# 1.10.1 release notes (iOS only)

*One file per version. Every App Store localisation has its own What's New and nothing inherits it. Every block was
checked by script (no dash, digit, exclamation, currency or percentage, no Premium, price, free, review, rating or
website, French no-break spaces and typographic apostrophes, real diacritics) and natively read.*

**Native read: done (2026-11-01).** A translator and an independent native reader per language, each checking the UI
names against that language's catalogue (the panel's heading is `ours.addTo` with `common.today`; the shortcut is
`widget.quickActionDumpTitle`), plus an English truth check against the fix (`ff4e2d9`). All four readers approved
their translator's text as written. "Schnellaktion" (German) and "acción rápida" (Spanish) are Apple's own names for a
home-screen quick action, chosen over words that would point to the Shortcuts app. French's no-break spaces were
lost in transit from the agents and restored by script before this file was written.

What 1.10.1 carries: one fix, iPhone only. Typing in the add panel on iOS 27, with Apple's keyboard, made Today's list
behind it climb a step per keystroke; it now stays still, however the panel was opened, and Close leaves no gap
(`ff4e2d9`, decision log 2026-11-01). **Android stays on 1.10.0:** the bug cannot happen there and no Android build was
made, so there is no Play paste. **Screenshots:** none to upload, because nothing on screen changed; App Store Connect
carries 1.10.0's over to the new version.

### English (377 characters)

```
On the newest version of iOS, typing in the Add to Today panel could make your list behind it creep up a step with every letter. That is fixed. Your list now stays exactly where it is while you type, whichever way you opened the panel: the + at the bottom, a share from another app, or the Brain dump shortcut. And closing the panel leaves no empty gap at the end of your list.
```

## Apple, App Review Information and notes

Sign-in required: **Yes**. User name `appreview@doubledone.app`. Password: `No password: one-time code, see notes`.
1.10.0's notes with the version paragraph replaced by 1.10.1's, and "A month on us" kept as its own shorter section,
since a new reviewer still meets it. 3928 characters, under App Store Connect's 4,000.

```
DoubleDone is a calm daily to-do app for people who find a full task list overwhelming. The daily loop is free and needs no account. Premium adds optional extras.

Version 1.10.1 fixes one bug. On iOS 27, typing in the Add to Today panel made the Today list behind it scroll up with every keystroke, however the panel was opened (the + button, a share from another app, or the Brain dump shortcut). The list now stays still while you type, and closing the panel leaves no gap at the end of the list. The fix is iPhone-only. There is no new feature and no change to purchases, data, accounts or AI.

A MONTH ON US (since 1.10.0)
A signed-in user with no Premium of any kind (no subscription and no free month running) may be given one month (30 days) of Premium for a note of two or more words (or one long word) from Settings > Send feedback, at most once every 180 days. The offer shows as a line under Send feedback, and once as a card on Today a week after the device first opens a version with it, only when our server says the account can get one (signed out, the line says a month follows sign-in). The month is granted on our server, the same way as the existing card-free month: no code, nothing renews, nothing is ever charged, and it ends by itself. It is never tied to a rating or a review, and the rating card stays off while such a month runs. With AI on, and only while a month is on offer, Claude checks the note once for spam (only a clear spam verdict stops it), and the screen says so above Send. After a note is sent, an optional link opens a public Google Calendar booking page for a 20-minute call with the developer. The review account already has Premium, so its note screen offers no month.

RATING CARD
The app asks for the App Store's rating card only through StoreKit's own API (requestReview), with no prompt, button or words of our own before or after it. It asks at most once per app version, at least 180 days apart, after a user closes a good day. It needs five closed days spread over at least two weeks, so it will not appear during review.

REVIEW ACCOUNT (sign-in is passwordless)
1. On Today, tap "Sync and sharing".
2. Enter appreview@doubledone.app and tap "Email me a code".
3. In any browser, open https://api.doubledone.app/review-code. Use the 6-digit code on the line that starts "appreview@doubledone.app:".
4. Type it into the app. Codes last an hour. If none shows, tap send again in the app and refresh the page.
This account already has Premium, so every Premium feature is open.

TO TEST THE IN-APP PURCHASE, PLEASE DO IT SIGNED OUT
The review account already has Premium, so it never sees the purchase screen. Buying needs no account: before signing in (or after Settings > Sign out), open Menu at the top right of Today, scroll to the end of the page, tap Premium, choose Monthly or Annual, and tap Go Premium. "Restore a purchase" is on the same screen.

AI AND THIRD-PARTY DISCLOSURE
Sort for me, Break it down and Scan send the text or photo the user chooses to Anthropic's Claude, only when the user taps one of them. A line naming Anthropic's Claude sits beside those buttons, the welcome offers an AI-free choice before anything runs, and Settings turns AI off entirely.

SHARED LIST (OURS)
A user can share one list with one other person by reading them a six-character code. There is no feed, discovery, profile or public content. "Report this list" and "Leave this list" are on the Ours screen, and we can disable any list server-side.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Settings > "Delete account and data". An Apple subscription is managed in the Apple ID settings, and the app says so before deleting.

DoubleDone is designed with ADHD, autistic and OCD users in mind, and its name now says who it is for. It is a to-do list, not medical care: it makes no medical, diagnostic or treatment claim, collects no health data, and its Terms of Use say so in a "Not medical care" section.
```
