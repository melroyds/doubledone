*One file per version. Play takes ONE box with locale tags, each at most 500 characters, so the English is written
short enough for the French. Every block is checked by script (no dash, digit, exclamation, currency or percentage, no

The UI names are the app's own, from each language's catalogue: `settings.reminderLabel`, `glance.channelName`,

block against the English, its catalogue's UI names and the rules, and all four tightened their text. German says

quotes « Clore la journée » rather than conjugating clore. Spanish keeps the list «discretamente» until the day ends,
and Italian says «in silenzio». Spanish, French and Italian now say fixes, not improvements, as the English does.
Written from the workflow's output, never retyped.

default, a third choice under Daily reminder), the widget in Today's own order and refreshed on every save, the AI
language fix (Chart a course, Reflect on this week and Scan answer in the app's language), the new one-colour
notification icon, R8 (nothing to announce), and every app fix since (CHANGELOG, Unreleased). Premium without an
account is iPhone only and is not mentioned here. The Apple What's New and the App Review notes follow the Play

## Apple, App Review Information and notes

Sign-in required: **Yes**. User name `appreview@doubledone.app`. Password: `No password: one-time code, see notes`.
1.10.1's notes with the version paragraph replaced by 1.10.7's, a line on signed-out purchases added to the purchase
section, and "A month on us" shortened to make room. 3988 characters, under App Store Connect's 4,000.

```
DoubleDone is a calm daily to-do app for people who find a full task list overwhelming. The daily loop is free and needs no account. Premium adds optional extras.

Version 1.10.7 lets a purchase made without an account reach the AI features. Apple lets an iPhone buy Premium without signing in, but Scan, Chart a course, Plan my day and Reflect on this week check Premium on our server, which needed an account, so they refused such a buyer. Now the app sends the purchase's anonymous RevenueCat id in a request header, our server confirms the purchase with RevenueCat, and those four work with no account (Guideline 5.1.1). The privacy policy describes this under "Premium without an account". They also answer in the app's language now. The rest are fixes: Hold me to it stops when its task leaves Today, the Add button shows a repeat's days in full, and sign-in no longer blames a good code when the connection drops. What is sold, and its prices, are unchanged.

A MONTH ON US (since 1.10.0)
A signed-in user with no Premium of any kind may get one month (30 days) of Premium for a short note from Settings > Send feedback, at most once every 180 days. Our server grants it: no code, nothing renews or is charged, and it ends by itself. It is never tied to a rating or a review, and the rating card stays off while such a month runs. With AI on, and only while a month is on offer, Claude checks the note once for spam, and the screen says so above Send. After a note is sent, an optional link opens a public Google Calendar page to book a 20-minute call with the developer. The review account already has Premium, so it is offered no month.

RATING CARD
The app asks for the App Store's rating card only through StoreKit's own API (requestReview), with no prompt, button or words of our own before or after it. It asks at most once per app version, at least 180 days apart, after a user closes a good day. It needs five closed days spread over at least two weeks, so it will not appear during review.

REVIEW ACCOUNT (sign-in is passwordless)
1. On Today, tap "Sync and sharing".
2. Enter appreview@doubledone.app and tap "Email me a code".
3. In any browser, open https://api.doubledone.app/review-code. Use the 6-digit code on the line that starts "appreview@doubledone.app:".
4. Type it into the app. Codes last an hour. If none shows, tap send again in the app and refresh the page.
This account already has Premium, so every Premium feature is open.

TO TEST THE IN-APP PURCHASE, PLEASE DO IT SIGNED OUT
The review account already has Premium, so it never sees the purchase screen. Buying needs no account: before signing in (or after Settings > Sign out), open Menu at the top right of Today, scroll to the end of the page, tap Premium, choose Monthly or Annual, and tap Go Premium. "Restore a purchase" is on the same screen. Since 1.10.7, a purchase made signed out also opens Scan, Chart a course, Plan my day and Reflect on this week, with no account.

AI AND THIRD-PARTY DISCLOSURE
Sort for me, Break it down and Scan send the text or photo the user chooses to Anthropic's Claude, only when the user taps one of them. A line naming Anthropic's Claude sits beside those buttons, the welcome offers an AI-free choice before anything runs, and Settings turns AI off entirely.

SHARED LIST (OURS)
A user can share one list with one other person by reading them a six-character code. There is no feed, discovery, profile or public content. "Report this list" and "Leave this list" are on the Ours screen, and we can disable any list server-side.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Settings > "Delete account and data". An Apple subscription is managed in the Apple ID settings, and the app says so before deleting.

DoubleDone is designed with ADHD, autistic and OCD users in mind, and its name now says who it is for. It is a to-do list, not medical care: it makes no medical, diagnostic or treatment claim, collects no health data, and its Terms of Use say so in a "Not medical care" section.
```
