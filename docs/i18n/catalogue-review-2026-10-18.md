Low priority. 3) Not in de.ts, but needs fixing before the German screenshots are made: the handoff fixtures (handoff-landing/design_handoff_landing/strings.json _fixtures) still use oursSeed.de.room „Nur wir“ (the catalogue's ours.presetJustUs is „Nur wir zwei“) and cast „Sam auf die Nachricht antworten“ (castSam is now „Auf Sams Nachricht antworten“). ## es

Verified in es.ts (not taken from the translator's list). Most serious first:

1. capture.aiNoteBoth is wrong: "Ordénalo tú, Divídelo y Escanear envían lo que escribes o fotografías a Claude, de Anthropic." "Ordénalo tú" means "sort it yourself", which is the no-AI path, so this privacy line names the opposite feature. "Divídelo" also does not match the button. It should use the real names, as capture.aiNote does: "Ordénalas por mí, Divídela en pasos y Escanear envían lo que escribes o fotografías a Claude, de Anthropic."

2. Ours is named three ways. ours.defaultName and the Menu say "Nuestra lista". ours.todayHeading says "Para hoy · Nuestro" and ours.noLongerOn says "ya no está en Nuestro". ours.shareTrim leaves the English "Ours" in the Spanish ("En Ours los títulos son más cortos..."). Pick one form, presumably "Nuestra lista", and fix all three.

3. Later has two names. today.laterHeading is "Más adelante", but breakdown.laterPhasesNote says "Estas esperan en Más tarde." It should say "Estas esperan en Más adelante." The landing uses "Más adelante".

4. Register split. The Ours strings use Spain-only vosotros forms, while the rest of the catalogue is neutral tú: ours.lead and welcome.sharedHeading ("cuidáis"), ours.sharedLine and welcome.sharedLead2 ("vuestros dos Hoy"), landing.faqShareA ("iniciáis"), ours.archive ("habéis tenido"), ours.whenToday/whenDay/whenRhythm/repeatNote ("veréis"), ours.reopened and ours.resumeCodeBody ("dejasteis"). A Latin American reader will notice. Decide whether the app is Spain-first or neutral. The landing stays neutral ("el Hoy de los dos", "cada uno inicia sesión"), using whatsnew.line3's own wording.

5. Pin has two Spanish names: premium.featurePin "Fija la única cosa del día" and welcome.premiumPinName "Fija lo más importante de hoy". The English differs too ("Pin the day's one thing" vs "Pin today's one thing"), so unify both languages together. The landing uses featurePin.

6. Typography: routines.exactHint uses straight double quotes ("Alarmas y recordatorios"), and today.holdSwapBody uses English curly quotes (“{title}”). The rest of the catalogue uses «», so both should be «Alarmas y recordatorios» and «{title}».

7. Not in the catalogue, but a picture must match: the handoff's _fixtures.oursSeed.es names the room "Solo nosotros", while ours.presetJustUs is "Solo tú y yo". Draw the Ours picture with "Solo tú y yo" so it matches altOurs and the app. Smaller point: the breakDownSteps fixture says "Entrar en la web de Hacienda", which leans toward Spain. Mexico also says Hacienda, but for wider reach the picture could say "Entrar en la web de impuestos".

Kept as they are, after checking: cardA11y says "La energía" (spoken, lower case) while altLow says "la Energía" (the UI label). Both read naturally and screen readers ignore case. castTaxes "Hacer la declaración" is natural in both Spain and Latin America. In faqFreeA, "Qué encaja ahora mismo" stays without ¿? and without «», capitalised as a name, as the spec asks.
