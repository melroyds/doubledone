# Section 4, step by step

**A = your iPhone. B = her Android.**

Three words used below:

- **The room** = the Ours list screen (Menu → Ours).
- **The DUE TODAY section** = the part further down your Today screen headed
  "DUE TODAY · <list name>". Rows there are the SHARED rows themselves, on loan to your day. They
  are not yours and you have not taken them on.
- **A brought copy** = a task you took from the room onto your own list. It sits in your normal
  list with a faint "· Ours" after the title.

Do these in order. Each one sets up the next.

---

## 4.1 Tick straight after reopening

1. On **A**, look at Today for the **DUE TODAY** section. If it is not there, go to the room, add a
   task, hold it, set it to repeat **daily**, then go back to Today. It appears within seconds.
2. **Do NOT bring it over.** You are going to tick it where it sits, inside that section. That is
   the whole point of this test: 4.1 ticks a SHARED row directly, and 4.2 ticks a copy you took.
   They are different code and they broke separately.
3. On **A**, swipe the app away into the background. Wait five seconds.
4. Open the app again.
5. **Immediately** tap the circle on that row, inside the DUE TODAY section. Do not wait.
6. Keep watching that circle for **ten seconds**. Do not tap anything else.

✅ **Pass:** it stays ticked.
❌ **Fail:** it un-ticks itself after a second or two.

---

## 4.2 Tick with no signal

1. On **A**, go to the room. Find a task with **no date and no repeat** (a plain one).
2. Press and hold it. Tap **Bring to my Today**.
3. Go to Today. You should see it in your normal list with "· Ours" after the title.
4. Turn on **aeroplane mode**.
5. Tick that row.
6. Turn aeroplane mode **off**. Wait thirty seconds.
7. Swipe the app away, then open it again.
8. Now look at **B**. Open the room there.

✅ **Pass:** that task is ticked on B.
❌ **Fail:** it is still un-ticked on B, and stays that way.

---

## 4.3 She ticks something you took

1. On **A**, go to the room. Pick a different plain task. Hold it, tap **Bring to my Today**.
2. Check it is on **A**'s Today, with "· Ours" after it. Leave it un-ticked.
3. On **B**, open the room and tick that same task.
4. On **A**, swipe the app away. Wait ten seconds. Open it again and go to Today.

✅ **Pass:** the row is **still on your Today**, now ticked. Then open Menu → Lookback and it is there
too.
❌ **Fail:** the row has disappeared from Today.

---

## 4.4 She removes something you took

1. On **A**, go to the room. Pick another plain task. Hold it, tap **Bring to my Today**.
2. Check it is on **A**'s Today with "· Ours". Leave it un-ticked.
3. On **B**, open the room. Press and hold that same task. Tap **Remove**.
4. On **A**, swipe the app away, wait ten seconds, open it again, go to Today.

✅ **Pass:** the row is still there, and underneath the title there is a small grey line reading
**"no longer on <your list name>"**.
❌ **Fail:** no line at all, or a line that mentions her by name.

---

## 4.5 The mark that says something changed

1. On **A**, open the room, look at it for a few seconds, then **leave the room** (go back to Today).
2. On **B**, open the room and **rename** any task (hold it, tap the title, change a word, save).
3. Wait twenty seconds.
4. On **A**, open the room again. **Start counting.**

✅ **Pass:** that row looks slightly warmer than the others, with a small line of words on it. After
about **eight seconds** it goes back to normal by itself.
❌ **Fail:** no mark at all, or it flickers, or it never goes away.

5. Now leave the room and open it again.

✅ **Pass:** no mark. That change has been seen.
❌ **Fail:** the mark is back for the same change.

---

## 4.6 Your own changes never get marked

1. On **A**, open the room and rename a task yourself.
2. Stay in the room. Look at it.
3. Leave the room and come back.

✅ **Pass:** no mark on the row you changed.
❌ **Fail:** your own edit is marked.

4. Now on **B**, rename that **same** task.
5. On **A**, leave the room and come back.

✅ **Pass:** now it IS marked.
❌ **Fail:** no mark. (This is the important half. A row must be able to mark more than once.)

---

## 4.7 A repeat on a day it is not due

1. On **A**, open the room. Add a task called "Bin night".
2. Hold it. Tap the repeat option. Choose **Weekly**, and pick a weekday that is **NOT today**.
   Save.
3. Stay in the room and look at that row.

✅ **Pass:** all three of these:
   - the row is **there** (it has not disappeared)
   - underneath it says its rhythm, something like "Weekly on Tue"
   - its circle is faded, with a small line saying **"you can tick this on its day"**

❌ **Fail:** the row vanished, or you can tick it today.

4. Try tapping the circle anyway.

✅ **Pass:** nothing happens.
❌ **Fail:** it ticks.

---
