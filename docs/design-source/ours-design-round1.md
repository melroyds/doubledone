# Handoff: DoubleDone "Ours" — a list two people keep (v1 board, for review)

## Status
First-pass design board for review and a v2 refinement prompt. Not yet a build contract — treat as the reference for critique.

## Files
- `DoubleDone Ours.html` — the board: all ten states in dark (D1–D10), the four most loaded in light (L1–L4), the four argued positions, the six-laws ledger, and the phase-3 card. Open in any browser; pan/zoom.

## The model (decided upstream, not redesigned)
One shared list, exactly two people, joined by a six-character code (`K7M-P4Q`, no 0/1/I/L/O) bound to one email address, valid once, for a day. Self-chosen names only. Nothing touches your Today unless you bring it there. Leaving freezes the list for both; nothing is deleted.

## The four argued positions (v1 stances)
1. **Naming first.** Create flow runs lightest → sharpest: purpose (the together-decision) → your name → their email (the one sharp edge) → the code. The screen that can fail comes last, beside the code it binds.
2. **Waiting is not a screen.** A waiting state is a read receipt by another name. Folded into the code screen as one line: "Nothing to watch here. You can close this — the list opens on its own the moment they join."
3. **Leaving stays visible.** On the resting screen's shelf (danger-soft, far corner), every visit. Hidden exits read as locked doors; the domestic threat model demands a visibly open one. Confirm card states the literal truth: freezes, both keep reading, nothing taken.
4. **The word stays "Ours."** Together = feature launch; The Shared List = clinical; Both = not a noun here; Us breaks in copy ("Us is closed"). Ours titles a screen and says the model.

## The ten states (dark frames)
- **D1 Nothing yet** — explains without selling; the Today promise + exit honesty up front; two EQUAL door cards (Start one / Join with a code) — the second seat is never subordinate.
- **D2 What's it for?** — chips: The shop · The house · Looking after someone · Just us · Type your own. Skip is a full bordered row ("Skip this — name it later"), first-class.
- **D3 Two small things** — self-chosen name ("Just a name you choose. Never your email.") + their email with the care sub: "The code will only work for this address, so it's worth a slow read. They'll never see yours."
- **D4 The code** — the one large thing (40px, 0.14em). "Made for {address} · works once, today only." Share it (fill) / Copy (outline) / "Wrong address? Make a fresh code" / the folded waiting line.
- **D5 They joined yours** — "Alex is here now." Serif, still, no celebration. Exit at the moment of doubt: "Not who you meant? Close the list."
- **D6 You joined theirs** — leads with the safety promise ("Nothing here lands on your Today unless you bring it over yourself"), then "Have a look," then "Not what you expected? Leaving takes nothing away."
- **D7 Resting (99% state)** — list name card ("the shop · Kept with Alex · since June" + Open the list), your editable chosen name, and the shelf: Close left, **Leave the list** danger-soft right.
- **D8 Closed (most loaded)** — "This list is closed now. Alex closed their side, and that closes it for both of you. Every word you both wrote is still here. Nothing was taken, and nothing moved." Sage inscription: "what was finished stays finished." Read the list (outline) / Put it away (tucks, deletes nothing).
- **D9 Joining with a code** — ONE large field, auto-caps, self-drawing hyphen (never six OTP boxes). Join dims in place until six characters. Gentle failure copy: "that one didn't open — worth checking the characters with them."
- **D10 The door on Today** — full shipped Today anatomy (load bar + caption, tools card with "now" chip, Right-now slot, motto). The door: one hairline row after the day's list, before the tools card — "Ours · the shop ›". **Never a number on it.** No shared list = no row at all; Ours lives in the Menu alone. No funnel.

Light (L1–L4): Nothing yet, The code, Resting, Closed — same anatomy re-tokened.

## The six laws → design answers
1. No attribution → shared rows are Today's rows to the pixel; done is sage and anonymous.
2. No counting → no number on the door, ever; no progress over two people.
3. No identifiers → only the code-maker sees the address they typed; the joiner sees a chosen name only.
4. Sealed room → nothing lists lists; the door names this list's purpose only.
5. Second seat may decline → equal doors (D1), promise-first join screen (D6), unhurried exits (D5/D6).
6. Never shame, doubled → shared rows carry no age, no "waiting on you," no nudge.

## Phase 3 stances (the shared list itself)
Reuse Today's grammar + held card minus AI actions, with three cuts stated now:
- **Drop "Mark as a lot"** — weight is private capacity language; on a shared row it reads as commentary on the other person's task.
- **Drop Pin** — one person's premium must never reorder what the other sees.
- **Add exactly one action: "Bring to my Today"** — the only bridge between rooms, by choice, one way, per person.

## Motion
House grammar throughout: 180ms rise + small settle; arrivals fade in as stills. Reduce-motion: the designed 90ms dissolve. **Nothing ever animates because of the other person** — no presence, no typing, no pulse on join. You find things changed when you look, like a kitchen table.

## Token usage (Dusk)
