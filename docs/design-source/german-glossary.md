# The German glossary (Phase 0 of the German version, 2026-08-08)

*The soul terms, transcreated before bulk translation so all 950 strings inherit consistent
bones. Register: warm informal **du**, never Sie. "DoubleDone" and "Settle" never translate.
Produced by the glossary agent, pending Melroy's veto; the translation panel is briefed with
this file verbatim.*

1. **Break it down → „Mach Schritte draus"** (feature reference: „Schritte draus machen"). The spoken „draus" is the warmth; „zerlegen/aufteilen" sound like DIY and splitting bills.
2. **Make it tiny → „Mach es winzig"**. „Winzig" keeps the near-comic smallness that grants permission.
3. **Lighten today → „Mach heute leichter"**. Bodily relief, and joins the „Mach"-family of AI buttons.
4. **Chart a course → „Kurs setzen"**. The native nautical idiom, button-length.
5. **Plan my day → „Plan meinen Tag"**. Keeps „mein" for ownership of the day.
6. **Sort for me → „Sortier für mich"**. The elided „Sortier" is the warm spoken register.
7. **The Lookback → „Der Rückblick"**. The fond „Jahresrückblick" glow, never the audit feel of „Verlauf".
8. **Scrapbook → „Erinnerungsalbum"** / **keepsake → „Andenken"**. „Andenken" is the small thing you keep because it holds a week.
9. **A lot → „viel"** / **Mark as a lot → „Das ist viel"** (state: „Als viel markiert."). The sentence a kind friend says; „schwer"/„groß" would judge.
10. **Pin → „Anpinnen"** (state: „Angepinnt ✓"). Messenger-era informal; „Anheften" reads like Windows.
11. **Today → „Heute"**. The established home-surface name, and the day-concept in one word.
12. **Focus on one thing → „Nur eine Sache"**. Builds the house rhythm where „nur" means permission to narrow.
13. **Close the day → „Feierabend machen"** (ritual name: „Feierabend"). The one German institution that already means you are allowed to stop now.
14. **Quiet → „Stille"**. A noun as an aesthetic promise.
15. **Rhythms → „Rhythmen"**. Something you live in, not something that fires.
16. **Brain dump → „Kopf leeren"**. Exactly the gesture; „Braindump" is consultant bingo.
17. **I also did that → „+ Hab ich auch gemacht"**. Spoken elision as warmth.
18. **Combine → „Zusammenlegen"**. Socks-and-towels concrete; „zusammenführen" smells of databases.
19. **Move up / Move down → „↑ Nach oben" / „↓ Nach unten"**. The arrows carry the verb.
20. **Breathing guide → „Atembegleitung"** (pill: „Atembegleitung · aus"). „Begleitung" walks beside you; „Anleitung" instructs.
21. **"Good morning. Just today." → „Guten Morgen. Nur heute."** The full stop is load-bearing: with „!" it becomes discount-banner German.

**(a) Task = die Aufgabe**, consistently, feminine agreement everywhere („die angepinnte Aufgabe", pronoun „sie"). „To-do" is flat denglisch. „Sache"/„Ding" allowed inside conversational lines, never as the structural noun.

**(b) Register notes.** Warmth is built with modal particles („ruhig", „mal", „erst mal", „einfach"), never exclamation marks: „Fang ruhig klein an." is warm, „Los geht's!" is a supermarket flyer. Kill nominal UI style („Hinzufügen einer Aufgabe"); let spoken elisions stand. Audit for Sie and impersonal „man" leaking in — one slip breaks the voice.

**(c) Affirmation style:** quiet permission in the „dürfen"-indicative, lowercase sentence-start (nouns keep their capitals), full stops only. Banned shame particles: „endlich", „schon wieder", „erst jetzt", „immer noch". Example, for "you're allowed to take this long.": **„so lange darf es dauern."**
