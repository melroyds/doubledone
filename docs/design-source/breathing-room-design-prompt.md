# Claude Design prompt — DoubleDone: the Breathing Room

Design a NEW surface for DoubleDone: a regulation room for the moment that precedes every other
tool in the app, the flooded nervous system. The person too activated to even look at their list.
Every existing tool assumes you can engage (plan the day, break down the task, lighten the load);
this is the one for when you cannot, yet.

## The product

DoubleDone is a calm, never-shame daily to-do app for adults with ADHD, autism, the AuDHD overlap
and OCD, live on web, the App Store and Google Play. The spine: **today is finite and achievable**;
the standing rule is **remove friction, never add a setting**. Brand is "Dusk": Newsreader serif for
headings, Atkinson Hyperlegible for body, a soft mauve (#946475) with sage (#7E9B6B), honey
(#C19A4F) and periwinkle (#6E72A0) as rare notes, warm paper (#FAF6F1) with a full dark mode
(#1B1917). The app already has a living time-of-day background; this room extends that visual
language rather than inventing a new one.

## What the room is (decided)

- **A blob that BREATHES, not a thing that moves.** Regulation, not stimulation: nothing to chase,
  read, or track. The blob swells and settles on a slow paced cycle (explore the pacing: something
  in the family of 4s in, brief hold, 6s out; longer exhale than inhale is the physiological point).
  The body entrains to rhythm; design the rhythm.
- **A haptic breath** on phones: a soft pulse marking the phases, so the rhythm reaches the body
  even with eyes closed or the phone face-down in a pocket.
- **A toggleable breathing GUIDE**: sparse paced words synced to the blob ("in…", "hold", "let
  go…" — find calmer words than these), for the person who needs teaching, invisible for the
  person who just needs company. One toggle, remembered, not a setting screen.
- **Sparse affirmations**: one at a time, long gaps, fading in and out, never popping, never
  stacking, never demanding to be read. RSD-safe wording (nothing that implies they failed to be
  calm). Explore whether affirmations belong in the same session as the guide, or alternate.
- **Silent v1.** No music, no soundscapes yet. The haptic is the audio. (Soundscapes are a later
  premium layer; do not design a player.)
- **The room NEVER locks.** The core room is free, forever, unlimited: money never gates a person
  in distress. Premium later buys richness (extra scenes/palettes in the family of the app's
  custom themes, soundscapes, a gentle trace in the Lookback), never the door. Do not design a
  paywall into these screens.

## Principles (the standing seven, plus the room's own)

1. Never shame. No timers counting, no session stats, no streaks, no "you did it!", no duration
   shown anywhere. Time is not a score here.
2. Predictable beats clever. The room looks the same every visit.
3. Calm over dense: this is the MOST whitespace-is-the-material surface in the app.
4. No new settings beyond the one guide toggle.
5. One-handed: exit is one obvious, always-visible, calm tap (the Focus screen's Exit discipline).
6. Reduce-motion honoured: the blob breathes by gentle opacity/soft-gradient pulse instead of
   scale, or goes still with only the haptic and the guide carrying the rhythm. Design this
   variant properly, not as an afterthought.
7. Photosensitivity-safe: slow, low-contrast, nothing that flashes, ever.
8. Entry is ALWAYS user-initiated. The app never diagnoses distress and never auto-opens this.

## Where it lives

- A tool in Today's constant frame panel (the fixed action layer: Plan my day, Focus, Lighten,
  Close the day, and now this). Naming is open: "Breathe" / "Settle" / "The breathing room" —
  propose and argue. It never occupies the clock-driven "Right now" slot.
- Offered gently on the evening wind-down surface, where the app already softens.

## Screens to produce (mobile, both dark and light Dusk)

1. The room mid-breath, at rest: the purest frame
2. The breathing guide ON, words synced to the blob
3. An affirmation moment (and how it coexists with or alternates with the guide)
4. The entry: the constant frame's panel with the new tool in place
5. The reduce-motion variant, designed with equal care
6. How a session ENDS well: no timer ran out, nothing to complete; the person just leaves, or the
   room offers a gentle settle-out. Design the leaving.

## What this is NOT

- Not a meditation app, a course, or a coach; no levels, programs, or progress
- Not a music player; no scrubbers, no track lists
- Not a modal ambush; entered on purpose, left in one tap
- Not gamified in any direction: no stats, streaks, badges, or history
- Never a paywall, a lock, a countdown, or a "time's up"
