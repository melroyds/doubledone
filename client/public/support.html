Support

DoubleDone is built and looked after by one person, and your message lands with him, not a ticket queue. Whatever it is, a bug, a question, or something that felt wrong, it is welcome.

Get in touch

Email support@doubledone.app and you will hear back, usually within a day or two. You can also send feedback from inside the app: open the Menu, then Settings, and use the feedback link near the bottom. Nothing you send is ever used for marketing.

Common questions

Where are my tasks? They live on your device. If you signed in and turned on sync, they also follow your account across devices. Signing out never deletes what is on the device.

Managing or cancelling Premium. If you subscribed on an iPhone or iPad, Apple handles it: open Settings on the device, tap your name, then Subscriptions. If you subscribed on our website or on Android, open the app's Premium screen and tap Manage subscription. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for, and your free monthly scrapbook stays either way.

Refunds. Purchases made through Apple are refunded by Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com. For purchases made on our website or on Android, email us within 7 days of the charge, see the terms for the details.

Deleting your account. In the app: Menu, Settings, then Delete account. It removes your synced data and keepsake images. Tasks stored only on your device stay yours.

Your data. The short version: no ads, no selling data, tasks stay yours. The long version is the privacy policy.
